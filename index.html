About PIS

Emvex builds PIS, software that works with the app to anticipate moments of emotional dysregulation. It is designed for people who experience emotional overload, and for the family and friends who support them.

PIS supports your own judgement. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and it does not replace professional care.

What PIS does

  • Real-time prediction

    PIS reads signals as they happen and flags rising tension early, giving you time to respond.

  • App integration

    PIS connects to the Emvex app, so prompts appear where you already are.

  • Privacy first

    You decide what is shared and with whom.
